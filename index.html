Introducing R4 Aerospace & Defence

Understanding Earth through coordinated satellite observations.

R4 Aerospace & Defence is developing satellite systems and analytical software to help organisations observe changing conditions on Earth, interpret events and make timely, evidence-based decisions.

Different sensors reveal different aspects of the same location: surface features, temperature, radar measurements and radio signals. When observations occur at different times, the scene may change between measurements. R4 is developing a coordinated three-satellite cluster to collect complementary observations within a common time window, alongside the ℝ Analytics Platform to analyse them together.

Coordinate observations

Coordinate multiple satellite sensors to examine the same area within a common observation window.

02 / ANALYSE

Interpret complementary evidence

Integrate complementary measurements to identify changes, assess events and quantify uncertainty.

03 / INFORM

Support operational decisions

Translate observations into interpretable evidence that supports civilian and defence decisions.

Intended applications: maritime monitoring, disaster assessment, environmental observation, critical infrastructure monitoring and defence operations.

The observation challenge

Dynamic events require coherent evidence.

Remote-sensing datasets often differ in acquisition time, geometry and environmental sensitivity. Correlating them can introduce uncertainty when the observed scene changes.

01 / TEMPORAL

Reduce temporal ambiguity

Coordinated acquisition is intended to reduce the displacement and scene evolution that complicate sequential observations.

02 / PHYSICAL

Combine complementary measurements

Radar, optical, thermal and cooperative radio-frequency observations characterise different physical properties.

03 / ANALYTICAL

Preserve evidence traceability

Spatial registration, provenance and uncertainty metadata provide context for interpreting a fused analytical result.

Complementary sensing

Four modalities.
Different physical insights.

Each modality contributes distinct evidence. Their integration is designed to improve interpretation while retaining the limitations of each measurement.

Sensing physics

Synthetic aperture radar

Radar backscatter provides structural and surface information under many conditions that constrain optical imaging. Coordinated receivers introduce additional observation geometries for multistatic processing.

Interpretation depends on frequency, resolution, geometry, sea state and calibration; radar does not establish identity independently.
