Applications

A common Earth-intelligence architecture for heterogeneous mission domains.

Application-specific analytical models convert synchronous multi-modal observations into operational indicators, alerts, evidence packages, and decision-support products.

Dual-use architecture

Observation is shared; interpretation is application-specific.

The underlying physics remains consistent across domains. SAR provides structural and all-weather information, optical imagery provides contextual classification, thermal sensing indicates energetic activity, and RF data contributes declared identity and movement metadata.

  • Configurable areas of interest, observation cadences, and event thresholds.
  • Domain-specific detectors, classification models, and risk indices.
  • Secure outputs for defence, government, humanitarian, scientific, and enterprise workflows.
