# R4 website content and interaction audit

Revision date: 30 October 2026.

## Scope and reference

The earlier review covered all nine pages in the supplied r4space.com navigation and sitemap. This revision retains those pages and adds the Platform and News & Events pages plus a local editorial utility.

The supplied Satleo reference, https://www.satleolabs.com/about-us/, informed the use of a chronological company narrative. Its founders, dates, achievements and wording were not transferred to R4. R4’s timeline begins with problem identification in 2023 and founder convergence in 2026, as explicitly supplied by the user. Subsequent stages retain planned/target labels.

## Substantive revisions

1. Added a global platform-demonstration request interface. It prepares an email for the visitor to review and send; no autonomous email transmission is implemented.
2. Expanded the observation-architecture comparison from four to fifteen dimensions. These cover modalities, acquisition, timing, dynamic-scene association, spatial registration, interpretation, displacement, environmental constraints, tasking, revisit, latency, uncertainty, engineering complexity, validation and application suitability.
3. Added a categorical figure with sensor modalities on the vertical axis and increasing synchronicity/temporal coordination on the horizontal axis. Coordinates are illustrative and non-numerical.
4. Added an educational displacement calculator using d = v × Δt. Its output is not a claimed mission error or R4 performance measurement.
5. Added an interactive six-stage R4 journey and explicit completed/current/planned distinctions.
6. Added a searchable news archive, announcement dialogs, filters, pagination and a data-export editor for continuing updates.
7. Generated eight conceptual illustrations and integrated them into the pages and galleries.
8. Replaced spacecraft-class labels with Satellite or three-satellite cluster terminology and removed trademark symbols from generated pages.
9. Retained development-stage qualification for orbital capabilities, latency, coverage, evidence and analytical uncertainty.

## Imagery and conceptual preview

All eight new image-generation outputs are used in the website. The original R4 logo is retained. Scene labels distinguish conceptual artwork from actual company hardware, facilities, event photography and sensor products. Image prompts and paths are documented in source/image-prompts.json.

The platform preview uses a generated maritime image, synthetic event records and explicitly labelled colour/contrast treatments. It is a frontend demonstration of interactions and evidence organisation, not the operational R Analytics processing stack. No measured target location, temperature, radar return or confidence score is implied.

## Publication and editorial boundaries

This delivery modifies downloadable files only. It does not change production hosting. The editor exports an updated dataset for review and deployment; it is not a remote authenticated CMS and does not continuously scrape external news. The email interface requires the visitor to send the prepared request. Validation coverage and remaining browser-verification limits are recorded in README.md.
