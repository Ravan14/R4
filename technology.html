Sensing physics

Complementary modalities constrain uncertainty.

Each modality contributes a distinct physical observation. Their synchronous combination improves interpretation by reducing the temporal and contextual disagreement that occurs across independent satellite passes.

  • Optical: morphology, colour, texture, object classification, and contextual interpretation.
  • SAR: all-weather structural response, surface roughness, displacement, and radar-coherent change.
  • Thermal infrared: heat distribution, combustion, equipment activity, and thermal anomalies.
  • Cooperative RF: declared identity, position, movement, and communication metadata from AIS, ADS-B, and APRS.
