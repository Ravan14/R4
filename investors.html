The system

Insight Set™ — Three Satellites. Multiple Sensors. One Intelligence Picture.

A proprietary three-satellite cluster designed for synchronous multi-modal Earth observation. Complementary optical, spectral, radar, thermal, and cooperative radio-frequency observations are integrated to provide comprehensive situational awareness within a common observation window.

01 · WHAT

What is happening?

Electro-optical and spectral observations characterise objects, materials, surface conditions, and observable environmental changes.

02 · WHERE

Where is it happening?

Synthetic Aperture Radar and geospatial observations provide location, structural context, and all-weather scene information.

03 · WHEN

When is activity occurring?

Thermal-infrared observations, acquisition timestamps, and temporal analysis characterise activity patterns and changes over time.

04 · WHO

Who may be involved?

Cooperative radio-frequency observations, including AIS, ADS-B, and APRS, support identification and movement correlation where compatible transmissions are available.

05 · WHY

Why does it matter?

The ℝ Analytics Platform integrates complementary evidence to evaluate anomalies, behavioural patterns, plausible explanations, and operational significance with associated confidence assessments.

From observations to intelligence

WHAT · WHERE · WHEN · WHO · WHY

Synchronous multi-modal observations provide complementary evidence of the same event. ℝ Analytics transforms this evidence into contextualised, confidence-qualified intelligence for time-critical decisions.
