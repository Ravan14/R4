Investor Overview

The gap between seeing and deciding is worth closing.

R4 Aerospace & Defence is developing a synchronous multi-modal Earth-intelligence system that transforms complementary satellite observations into decision-oriented intelligence for defence, civil, and commercial operations.

We are raising US$2 million in pre-seed capital to accelerate technology development, engineering validation, and commercial readiness.

The Thesis

From fragmented observations to integrated intelligence.

Conventional Earth-observation systems often generate independent datasets with different acquisition times, sensing limitations, and processing workflows. This fragmentation can delay corroboration and reduce operational awareness.

The Gap

Isolated Observations

Individual sensing modalities provide valuable but incomplete evidence. Optical, radar, thermal, and radio-frequency observations each have distinct limitations.

The Gap

Temporal Discontinuity

Conventional tip-and-cue operations may require sequential acquisitions. Objects and environmental conditions can change between observation events, complicating reliable interpretation.

The Gap

Delayed Intelligence

Disconnected acquisition, processing, and analytical workflows can increase the interval between event observation and operational decision-making.

R4 aims to transform isolated observations into timely, correlated, and decision-ready Earth intelligence.

The System

Insight Set — Three Satellites. Multiple Sensors. One Intelligence Picture.

The Insight Set is a proprietary three-satellite cluster designed to coordinate complementary Earth observations within a common observation window.

Integrating optical, spectral, Synthetic Aperture Radar, thermal-infrared, and cooperative radio-frequency evidence, the system aims to improve situational awareness and reduce temporal information gaps.

01 · WHAT

What is happening?

Electro-optical and spectral observations characterise visible objects, material properties, surface conditions, and environmental changes.

02 · WHERE

Where is it happening?

Synthetic Aperture Radar and geospatial observations provide location, structural context, and information under conditions that constrain conventional optical sensing.

03 · WHEN

When is activity occurring?

Thermal-infrared evidence, acquisition timestamps, and temporal analysis support the identification of activity patterns and changes across observation periods.

04 · WHO

Who may be involved?

Cooperative radio-frequency observations, including AIS, ADS-B, and APRS, contribute declared identification and movement information where compatible transmissions are available.

05 · WHY

Why does it matter?

Multi-modal analytical inference evaluates anomalies, behavioural patterns, plausible explanations, and operational significance using corroborated evidence and confidence estimates.
