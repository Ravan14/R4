Investor overview

The gap between seeing and deciding is worth closing.

R4 is building a satellite system that delivers fused, decision-ready Earth intelligence in minutes, not days. We are raising a pre-seed round to de-risk the software and autonomy stack before we commit to flight hardware.

The thesis

Minutes, not days.

Strategic decision-makers still rely on slow, fragmented Earth observation. Data arrives late, sensors work in silos, and the operational picture is incomplete when it matters most.

The gap

Sensors work alone

Cameras fail at night and under cloud. Radar sees shape but not identity. Cooperative tracking misses "dark" targets that switch their beacons off. No single sensor delivers a complete picture.

The gap

Workflows are slow and manual

Raw imagery is downloaded, stored, and processed on the ground. Tasking and cross-cueing are largely manual — so answers arrive in hours or days.

The gap

Operational risk compounds

Dark assets go untracked, damage assessment runs late, and situational awareness drops exactly when operational tempo rises.

R4 closes the gap: fused, decision-ready intelligence in minutes, not days.

The system

The Insight Set: one microsatellite, two CubeSats, four sensors.

A purpose-built three-satellite cluster for synchronous multi-modal collection and fusion — designed from day one for real-time intelligence delivery.

On orbit

One microsatellite

Hyperspectral and thermal imaging provide the "what": what a surface is made of and how hot it is, day and night.

On orbit

Two CubeSats

Radar plus the beacons that ships, aircraft, and radio operators already broadcast (AIS, ADS-B, APRS) provide the "where, when, and who": all-weather change detection and declared movement.

Unified software

ℝ Analytics Platform

One software stack turns raw sensor readings into mission-ready intelligence: a data platform, operational dashboards, and constellation control.

Why now

Designed for fusion from the first line of code.

Readiness below uses the NASA TRL scale — technology readiness levels that track a capability from concept (1) to flight-proven (9).

TRL 3 — software engineering models

ℝ AI Engine

An architecture that fuses what the four sensors see — and lets the sensors cue each other automatically while cutting what must be sent to the ground.

TRL 3 — concept and design

Autonomous formation control

An indigenous guidance, navigation, and control stack for formation flying and collision avoidance — minimising ground intervention.

PDR-level payload designs

Multi-modality by design

Synchronous collection from a coordinated cluster — architected as one integrated system, not stitched-together sensors.

Market and traction

A $100B+ geospatial intelligence ecosystem.

R4 focuses on high-value defence, intelligence, and critical-infrastructure use cases. Early interest has come from government and defence stakeholders in "dark target" tracking and real-time battle-damage-assessment pilots.

Why R4, why now
  • Fusion-first architecture — not a retrofitted imaging business.

  • Capital-efficient: software, HIL, and payload engineering models come before flight hardware.
