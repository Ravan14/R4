Example: the morning after the storm

One operating picture, before the clouds clear.

A storm has passed and clouds still cover the region. A camera alone sees nothing. R4's radar maps the flood extent through the clouds; the heat sensor finds lingering fires and running generators; the camera fills in detail wherever the sky clears; radio data tracks ships and aircraft in the area. Combined, they give response teams one operating picture — hours before a single-sensor approach could.

  • Flood extent and severity, in hours
  • Which roads and bridges are still open
  • Status of hospitals, power, and water
  • Prioritised maps for response coordination
