Defence & Security

Watch what matters. Day and night. Even when someone hides.

R4 gives defence and security teams a persistent, all-weather view of sea lanes, borders, and strategic sites — with evidence that is verified the moment it is collected, not hours later.

How it works

Four sensors answer the question together.

Defence decisions depend on telling declared activity from observed behaviour. Here is how the four sensors answer four questions.

Observation questionPrimary evidenceCorroborating evidencePotential output
Is something there?Radar sees structureCamera or heat confirmsA flagged location with a confidence score
Is its identity real?Radio beacons say who it claims to beThe other sensors check position and behaviourAn identity-consistency check
Has activity changed?Radar and camera images over timeHeat or radio activity shiftsA change map with an event label

Mission domains

What R4 watches for you

High-level, non-tactical capability areas — developed further through mission studies with your team.

Maritime

Dark vessels and busy sea lanes

Track ships that switch off their radios, and see the ports, routes, and offshore sites they use.

  • Non-cooperative and dark-vessel indications
  • Rendezvous and loitering patterns
  • Port and sea-lane activity
Land

Borders and corridors

Watch designated border regions and access routes for change — in any weather.

  • Change over time, route by route
  • Temporary structures and mobility indicators
  • Weather-independent radar coverage
Aerospace

Airfields and flight activity

Monitor designated airfields, runway use, aircraft presence, and heat activity — and check identities against transponders.

  • Transponder consistency analysis
  • Infrastructure and apron change
  • Activity-state indicators
Strategic

Critical infrastructure

Keep persistent watch over selected energy, transport, communications, and logistics sites.

  • Structural and land-use change
  • Thermal anomaly detection
  • Operational continuity indicators
Intelligence support

Wide-area situational awareness

Give analysts a prioritised feed of corroborated changes — not raw imagery.

  • Object and activity detection
  • Evidence-linked alerting
  • Confidence and uncertainty metadata
Humanitarian

Defence support to civil authorities

Support disaster response with rapid flood maps, access routes, and infrastructure status.

  • Rapid flood and damage mapping
  • Access and logistics assessment
  • Common operating picture support
Sovereign by design

Fits the systems and rules you already have.

Outputs are delivered as alerts, map layers, imagery, change maps, summaries, and machine-readable feeds — and can be deployed under sovereign data custody, with controlled access and full auditability.
