Defence & Security

Sovereign Earth intelligence for persistent operational awareness.

Synchronous multi-modal observation can support high-level monitoring, anomaly identification, change assessment, and evidence-based decision support across maritime, border, strategic, and humanitarian defence missions.

Operational rationale

Corroboration across sensing modalities strengthens confidence.

Defence interpretation frequently depends on distinguishing declared activity from observed behaviour. Synchronous SAR, optical, thermal, and cooperative RF observations provide mutually constraining evidence.

Potential applications

Defence and security mission domains

These are high-level, non-tactical application categories intended for capability communication and mission-study development.

Maritime

Maritime domain awareness

Correlate detected vessels with cooperative transmissions, identify route or behaviour anomalies, observe maritime corridors, and monitor port or offshore activity.

  • Non-cooperative or dark-vessel indications
  • Rendezvous and loitering analysis
  • Port, berth, and sea-lane activity
Land

Border and corridor monitoring

Observe changes in designated border regions, access corridors, temporary structures, mobility indicators, and terrain-dependent activity patterns.

  • Multi-temporal change assessment
  • Route and infrastructure observation
  • Weather-independent radar coverage
Aerospace

Airfield and aviation activity

Monitor designated airfields, runway utilisation, aircraft presence, thermal activity, and correlation with cooperative aviation signals.

  • ADS-B consistency analysis
  • Infrastructure and apron change
  • Activity-state indicators
Strategic

Critical and strategic infrastructure

Persistent observation of selected energy, transport, communications, industrial, and logistics infrastructure for activity and condition changes.

  • Structural and land-use change
  • Thermal anomaly detection
  • Operational continuity indicators
ISR support

Wide-area situational awareness

Prioritise areas of interest, identify statistically significant changes, and provide analysts with corroborated geospatial evidence.

  • Object and activity detection
  • Evidence-linked alerting
  • Confidence and uncertainty metadata
Humanitarian

Defence support to civil authorities

Support disaster response, search-area prioritisation, route accessibility, infrastructure status, and humanitarian logistics during major emergencies.

  • Rapid flood and damage mapping
  • Access and logistics assessment
  • Common operating picture support
Sovereign integration

Designed to complement existing command and geospatial systems.

Outputs can be structured as alerts, geospatial layers, imagery products, change maps, analytical summaries, and machine-readable interfaces. Deployment architecture may be configured for sovereign data custody, controlled access, and institutional security requirements.
