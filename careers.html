Careers

Build the eyes that watch over Earth.

Join a team building satellites, sensing science, and the software that turns observations into decisions.

Disciplines

Where you can contribute

Open roles vary with programme phase. These disciplines show where we recruit, intern, and collaborate.

Satellite systems engineer

Design the satellites end to end: architecture, requirements, interfaces, budgets, and verification.

Payload engineer

Build the sensors — radar, camera, heat, and radio — and own calibration, characterisation, and validation.

Guidance, navigation, and control engineer

Make the three satellites fly in formation: relative navigation, attitude control, autonomy, and mission simulation.

RF and communications engineer

Own the links: space-to-ground radios, onboard networks, spectrum engineering, and modem integration.

Remote-sensing scientist

Turn raw sensor data into science: geospatial algorithms, calibration, validation, and application research.

AI and machine-learning engineer

Teach the platform to see: multi-sensor fusion, object detection, anomaly analysis, and prediction.

Geospatial data engineer

Build the data backbone: ingestion, catalogues, tiling, STAC and OGC services, and scalable pipelines.

Cloud and platform engineer

Run the platform: distributed storage, orchestration, GPU workloads, APIs, and deployment automation.

Cybersecurity engineer

Protect the mission: threat modelling, identity, key management, secure architecture, and auditability.

Business development

Find the missions: customer discovery, institutional partnerships, pilots, proposals, and strategic accounts.

Space policy and regulation

Navigate the rules: licensing, spectrum, remote-sensing regulation, export control, and data policy.

Research and engineering internships

Structured projects in mission analysis, remote sensing, AI, simulation, software, and market research.

What you'll work on

Hard problems, real outcomes.

Spacecraft that fly together

Formation flying, payload integration, and mission operations for a three-satellite cluster.

Sensing that corroborates itself

Radar, camera, heat, and radio fusion — from calibration to applied science.

Software that decides

AI, satellite-data pipelines, and secure platforms that turn observations into decisions.

Candidate profile

Scientific rigour and systems-level reasoning.

Evidence-based

You form hypotheses, test them, and document uncertainty honestly.

Interdisciplinary

You connect spacecraft, sensing physics, data systems, and customer goals.

Mission-oriented

You translate engineering work into measurable operational value.

Responsible

You apply security, safety, legal, and ethical rigour throughout development.

Expression of interest

Send a short profile, your evidence, and the discipline you want to contribute to.

Email applications and research-collaboration proposals to careers@r4space.com. Include a curriculum vitae, portfolio or publication links, location, availability, and preferred engagement type.
